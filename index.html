All In Labs

All In Labs is an independent engineering and research lab in London, run by Jonny Spicer. We believe transformative AI systems are almost upon us, and we're working to seamlessly integrate such systems into the rest of our lives.

Selected Client Work

  • A real-time AI assistant for facilitated CBT phone conversations for Kaya Guides, including evaluation and deployment. Featured in the Cloudflare blog.
  • Tech lead for Offworld Labs on the RETINA project, a mesh network of consumer-grade passive radars capable of detecting objects up to 60km away with hardware costing less than $500.

Selected Other Projects

  • Kerouac, a self-hosted daily writing studio.
  • Boudica, an experimental chess engine built mostly by prompting Claude Code. It plays at roughly 2100 Elo.
  • Who Sucks?, a conflict journal that walks you through an interpersonal conflict in five stages, based on the blog post “The facts; You suck; I suck; Everybody sucks; Nobody sucks”.
  • Alien Epistemology, a cause-prioritisation tool built on the Drake equation.
