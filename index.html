All In Labs

All In Labs is an independent engineering and research lab in London, run by Jonny Spicer. We believe transformative AI systems are almost upon us, and we're working to ensure these systems become integrated into our lives in positive ways.

Selected Client Work

  • A real-time AI assistant for facilitated CBT phone conversations for Kaya Guides, including evaluation and deployment. Featured in the Cloudflare blog.
  • Tech lead for Offworld Labs on the RETINA project, a mesh network of consumer-grade passive radars capable of detecting objects up to 60km away with hardware costing less than $500.

Selected Other Projects

  • Kerouac, a minimalist writing studio that empowers you to write consistently and build a coherent body of work.
  • Boudica, a classical chess engine that plays at roughly 2100 Elo. Mostly an experiment in asking coding agents to hillclimb on improving ELO.
  • Who Sucks?, a guided journalling exercise for resolving conflict in five stages, based on this blog post.
  • Alien Epistemology, a Fermi estimation tool for thinking about funding searches for extraterrestrial intelligence.
