It's been a busy week at the lab, with a mixture of work on streamlining internal systems as well as bigger-picture strategy for the future.

On the former, Claude is now a pretty solid personal assistant; it's trivial to add an MCP server to any new tool that gets built, or indeed any other tool in your workflow (this is doubly true given Claude is also great at reverse-engineering undocumented APIs). Combined with Notion and scheduled tasks, this has really made Claude feel like an "everything" app for me this week.

On the latter, I have been working on a piece that considers what happens with AI "by default", by taking some (in my opinion reasonable) assumptions and then extrapolating what might happen if these assumptions and current trends continue to hold. The results so far suggest a great deal of instability in the next 3-4 years, which is concerning. I'm hopeful that when I publish the post, it will become apparent that I was wrong in some way, which is an odd feeling to have towards a piece of work.
