While it was tempting to vibe-code some kind of spectacular animation, I am already sick of the way that Claude Design-designed sites looked, so instead drew inspiration from programmer-blogger heavyweight [Dan Luu](https://danluu.com) and my second-favourite company in the latest Entrepreneur First cohort, [Pragma](https://pragma.inc/). For those curious, my favourite company is of course my dear friends over at [Lokantara](https://lokantara.space).

The web will increasingly be used by agents and not humans, and so fancy design is irrelevant. One of our core values at All In Labs is focus. It sounds trite to say this -- nobody aspires to be unfocused -- but the reality is that the overwhelming majority of people and organisations cannot hold their focus for long, due to it requiring a pathological ability to ignore outside pressures.

This will be All In Labs' north star moving forwards; focus. Focus on the most important problems, and focus on driving towards the best solutions, even when doing so requires humility and perseverance.
